The trade-off

Tracking first.Onboarding later.

01

What I chose

Ship portfolio tracking, not full onboarding. A view of what a user already holds, and a reason to open it every morning.

02

Onboarding inside the partner app. Anyone who decided to invest had to leave and finish on ours.

No KYC data changed hands, legal cleared much faster, and the channel scaled to 17,000+ users tracking ₹7,000 Cr. The loops on top lifted repeat engagement 38%.

Your questions

Before the first conversation.

Why tracking before onboarding? +

Tracking meant partners shared no KYC data, so legal review cleared much faster. It gave people a reason to open it every morning, and the loops built on top did the rest.

How did you work with three partner teams? +

As the single PM across three external engineering teams and our own squads. None of them reported to me, so most of the job was getting one spec agreed and shipped on the same date.

Where did compliance come in? +

SEBI compliance guardrails were part of what I owned, alongside the PRDs, wireframes and SDK specs. Scoping the MVP as tracking meant no KYC data changed hands, which is why legal cleared it much faster.
