about

i'm a product manager at dezerv. six years in, mostly fintech, and i came up through growth, so i've never been able to separate "is this good?" from "will anyone find it?" to me that's one question.

i care most about distribution: where people already spend their time, and what it takes to meet them there. i'd rather put a small product inside an app millions already open than build a big one nobody finds. that's why dezerv now lives inside moneycontrol, livemint and inshorts instead of paying for every user.

i learned this the expensive way. when placement season passed me by in 2020, i built seven online stores and watched ads eat my savings before the sales showed up. learning seo on those dead stores got me my first job, and i've been working on the same question ever since.

the long version
the problem
three partners, three engineering teams that don't report to me, and a regulator watching. every extra screen in the spec was another legal review and another month.
what i chose
ship portfolio tracking, not full onboarding. a view of what a user already holds, and a reason to open it every morning.
what i gave up
onboarding inside the partner app. anyone who decided to invest had to leave and finish on ours.
what happened
no kyc data changed hands, legal cleared much faster, and the channel scaled to 17,000+ users. the loops on top did the rest.
