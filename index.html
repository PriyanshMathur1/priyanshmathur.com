Judging paid and organic separately, each on its own numbers.

Organic grew from 10K to 140K sessions a month and now brings 29% of new eligible lead signups.

Before you hire me

What you may want to know.

What are you best at? +

Distribution. Finding where people already spend their time, and building the thing that meets them there. Plus the unglamorous half: getting three companies who don’t report to me to ship one spec on the same date.

How big was your team? +

On the embedded channel I was the single PM across three partner engineering teams and our own squads. None of them reported to me, so most of the job was getting one spec agreed and shipped, inside SEBI’s rules.

How technical are you? +

A BCA in computer science, SQL and Python most weeks, and enough hands-on building that engineers argue with me about the right things. I wrote the PRDs for Dezerv’s page system and worked through rendering and page speed with engineering.

Paid or organic? +

Both, judged on blended CAC. But I learned the expensive way that paid attention is rented: ads ate my savings on my own stores. Organic is what eventually made one of them work.

What are you not? +

I’ve led individual contributors and external teams, not an org of PMs. And I’m not a design-led PM. Better you hear it here than in round four.

What do you want next? +

Deeper product ownership in fintech and wealth. I’ve done 0 to 1 inside someone else’s app. Next, I’d like to do it on a surface the company owns.
