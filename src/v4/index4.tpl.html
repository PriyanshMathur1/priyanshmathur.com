    01 / Embedded distribution↗

    Dezerv: build on a road people already take.

    Product management, fintech, partnerships

    Nobody wakes up wanting another wealth app. So instead of asking people to download one, we put a portfolio tracker inside Moneycontrol, Livemint and Inshorts.

    17,000+ users. 700 qualified leads a month. 75% lower CAC than paid channels.

    Read the decisions

    The choiceVersion one tracked portfolios. That's it. No full onboarding, no grand tour. A smaller compliance surface, faster partner sign-off, and a reason to come back tomorrow.

    My jobSole PM across three external partner engineering teams. In 2025 I took full ownership of the embedded channel.

    What made it stickDaily portfolio movement, plus news tied to the holdings you actually own. Repeat engagement went up 38%. Lead scoring lifted lead-to-call by 21%.

    The fine printThe product tracked ₹7,000 Cr in assets. The partner apps reach 20M+ people combined; that is their audience, not our user count. Tracked is not managed.

  • 02 / Organic growth↗

    Wint Wealth: answer the question, then point at the product.

    Investor tools, content operations, SEO

    I owned the investor tools: the Bonds Directory, FD calculators, gold and silver rates. The order we built them in mattered as much as the traffic they brought.

    7K to 153.4K monthly organic sessions, one year apart.

    Read the decisions

    The choiceBonds Directory first. It connected what people were searching for with what Wint actually sold. Everything else queued behind it.

    The machineFive editors, seven subject-matter experts and a few agencies. Named authors, expert review and careful financial language, at 500+ articles a month.

    The fine printTools and content together, so not a controlled experiment proving one feature did it all. Shared zero baseline on the chart, two measured points, no invented trend line.

    03 / Side build↗

    Loom: a production ledger for a bed-cover factory.

    Solo build, Postgres, Next.js, Supabase

    Replaced the workbook my father's factory had run on for years with a phone-first ledger that answers his only question, where did that quantity go, and imported the real register so nothing had to be retyped.

    Stock is never edited, only moved. The rules live in Postgres, so the books cannot fail to balance.
