The story

Growth taught meto build product.

I care most about distribution: where people already spend their time, and what it takes to meet them there. I’d rather put a small product inside an app millions already open than build a big one nobody finds. That’s why Dezerv now lives inside Moneycontrol, Livemint and Inshorts, instead of paying for every user.

I learned this the expensive way. When placement season passed me by in 2020, I built online stores and watched ads eat my savings before the sales showed up. Learning SEO on those stores got me my first job.

I started as the intern who cold-messaged the owner of an Australian agency, with no experience and no portfolio. I ended up leading the team. Most of how I handle stakeholders today, I learned on those client calls.

From an idea to a channel

Find. Scope. Ship.

01

Find where people already are.

Before building anything, find the surface your users open every day, and what would make them open yours.

02

Scope the smallest version.

Cut the spec until legal can clear it fast and it still teaches you something. Portfolio tracking, not full onboarding.

03

Ship, then build the loops.

Launch, then give people a reason to come back: daily movement, insights, news-linked triggers. That lifted repeat engagement 38%.

Before you hire me

What you may want to know.

What are you best at? +

Distribution. Finding where people already spend their time, and building the thing that meets them there. Plus the unglamorous half: getting three companies who don’t report to me to ship one spec on the same date.

How big was your team? +

On the embedded channel I was the single PM across three partner engineering teams and our own squads. None of them reported to me, so most of the job was getting one spec agreed and shipped, inside SEBI’s rules.

How technical are you? +

A BCA in computer science, SQL and Python most weeks, and enough hands-on building that engineers argue with me about the right things. I wrote the PRDs for Dezerv’s page system and worked through rendering and page speed with engineering.

Paid or organic? +

Both, judged on blended CAC. But I learned the expensive way that paid attention is rented: ads ate my savings on my own stores. Organic is what eventually made one of them work.

What are you not? +

I’ve led individual contributors and external teams, not an org of PMs. And I’m not a design-led PM. Better you hear it here than in round four.

What do you want next? +

Deeper product ownership in fintech and wealth. I’ve done 0 to 1 inside someone else’s app. Next, I’d like to do it on a surface the company owns.
